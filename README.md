## 🚧 Featured Project: [Traffic Engineering Toolkit](https://github.com/shreyanspeaking-arch/Traffic-Engineering-Problems)

A collection of Python programs that solve core traffic and highway engineering problems. Results are exported to Excel and visualised with Matplotlib.

| # | Module | What it does |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | 🚗 **Cordon Count Study** | Tracks vehicle accumulation inside a cordon area from entry/exit counts |
| 2 | 🛣️ **Curve Fitting for Highways** | Designs parabolic vertical curves and checks superelevation and safe speed per **IRC:73-2023** |
| 3 | 💰 **Highway Economics** | Cost-benefit analysis and **NPV** appraisal of highway upgrades |
| 4 | 📶 **LOS Estimation** | Level of Service (A–F) for multilane highways per **HCM 1994** |
| 5 | 🚌 **Modal Split Analysis** | Gravity-model trip distribution, **Multinomial Logit** mode choice, and Greenshields / Greenberg / Underwood traffic stream models |
| 6 | 🗺️ **Network Study Plans** | Control/coverage count expansion and **Furness** O-D matrix balancing |
| 7 | ⏳ **Queuing Analysis** | D/D/1, M/D/1, M/M/1 and M/M/N queues, plus cumulative arrival-departure curves |
| 8 | ⏱️ **Speed Studies** | Space and time mean speed, spot-speed statistics, and chi-square goodness-of-fit tests |
| 9 | 📈 **Traffic Flow Parameters** | Daily/monthly variation factors, **AADT** and annual vehicle-km travelled |
| 10 | 🦺 **Traffic Safety Analysis** | **HSM** crash prediction at signalized intersections and safe stopping distance comparison |
| 11 | 🚦 **Traffic Volume Studies** | ADT, AWT and AADT/AAWT/AAWeT computation with trend plots |
